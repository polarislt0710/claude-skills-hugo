# Growth Strategies

Picking the RIGHT channel matters more than executing a wrong one well. Playbooks differ for B2B SaaS vs. Consumer.

## B2B SaaS Growth (5 channels)

1. **Content SEO**
   - Long-tail PROBLEM-focused keywords (`"how to do X with Y"`)
   - 2-3 anchor pillar pieces + clusters of supporting articles
   - Internal linking + topical authority over time
   - Slow but compounds (12+ months)

2. **Developer marketing**
   - Open-source companion repo / SDK / examples
   - First-class docs (better than competitors)
   - API-first product surface
   - Developer-targeted channels: Hacker News, dev.to, lobste.rs, /r/programming

3. **Product-led growth (PLG)**
   - Free tier with REAL value (not crippled)
   - Friction-free onboarding (≤ 3 minutes to first "wow")
   - Upgrade trigger tied to actual usage (e.g., team size, projects)
   - Time-to-value > sales narrative

4. **Outbound (signal-based)**
   - Trigger on hiring signals (new role X = need for tool X)
   - Funding rounds, tech stack changes, exec hires
   - Personal, specific, ≤3 sentence first message
   - NOT generic spray-and-pray

5. **Community**
   - Build where the audience IS (don't drag them to your Discord)
   - Discord / Slack / Circle / sub-forum / etc.
   - Champion model: find 5 power users, invest deeply

## Consumer App Growth (4 mechanics)

1. **Referral loops**
   - Both giver AND receiver get something
   - Trigger refer at moment-of-value (post-success, not post-signup)
   - Track activation, not invitations

2. **UGC (User-Generated Content) triggers**
   - Make outputs INHERENTLY shareable (watermark = sharing tax, OK if value > tax)
   - Native share to platforms users already love
   - Templates / customization → vanity sharing

3. **Creator partnerships**
   - **Micro influencers (10k–100k followers) >>> macro (1M+)**
   - Authentic creators in your niche
   - Equity / rev-share / product credit > flat fee for early stage

4. **Virality mechanics**
   - **Content viral**: artifact spreads on its own merit (TikTok recipe, meme template)
   - **Invite viral**: product useless solo, useful in groups (Slack, Calendly, splitwise)
   - Pick ONE — don't fake the other

## How to choose
- **Solo dev / small team / B2B**: Content SEO + Developer marketing first.
- **B2B w/ self-serve**: PLG before outbound.
- **B2B mid-market / enterprise**: Outbound + Community.
- **Consumer**: Pick 1 referral mechanic + 1 content/creator strategy.
- **NEVER do all 5 — pick 1, exhaust it, then add a 2nd.**
