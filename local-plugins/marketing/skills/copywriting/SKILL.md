# Copywriting Frameworks

Three battle-tested frameworks. Pick the one that fits the format and the audience's emotional state.

## AIDA — Attention, Interest, Desire, Action

**Best for**: Ads, landing page hero sections, video scripts.
**Audience state**: Doesn't know your product yet.

```
Headline (Attention):    [counter-intuitive or pattern-break]
Body — Interest:         [the relevant promise — what they get]
Body — Desire:           [proof / story / specificity]
CTA (Action):            [one verb, ≤4 words]
```

## PAS — Problem, Agitate, Solution

**Best for**: Email subject lines, ad headlines, landing page openers.
**Audience state**: Has the pain but hasn't named it yet.

```
Problem:   State the pain in their own words
Agitate:   Make them feel the cost of inaction (1-2 lines max)
Solution:  Position your product as the answer (1 line)
```

## FAB — Features, Advantages, Benefits

**Best for**: Product descriptions, demos, comparison pages.
**Audience state**: Knows the category, comparing options.

```
Feature:     What it IS               (e.g., "OAuth login")
Advantage:   What it DOES BETTER      (e.g., "no password to forget")
Benefit:     What it MEANS for them   (e.g., "ship 30 minutes faster Monday morning")
```

## Universal copywriting rules

1. **Specificity beats vague.** "47% faster" beats "much faster"
2. **Address the reader as "you."** Never "the user".
3. **Benefits over features.** Always.
4. **Short sentences. Short paragraphs.**
5. **Every word earns its place.** Cut, then cut again.
