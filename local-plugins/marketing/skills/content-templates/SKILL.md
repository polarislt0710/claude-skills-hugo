# Content Templates

Battle-tested structural templates for the 3 most common marketing content surfaces.

## Landing Page Copy

```
HERO
  - Headline:      [Clear value prop, <10 words]
  - Subheadline:   [Who it's for + what they get]
  - CTA:           [Verb-first action: "Start Free", "Get Access"]

SOCIAL PROOF       [logos / numbers / 1 testimonial]

FEATURES (3)       [each: benefit-first, single sentence, icon optional]

FAQ (3)            [answer the 3 biggest objections, NOT random questions]

FINAL CTA          [repeat hero CTA + urgency element]
```

## Email Marketing

### Subject line formulas
- **Curiosity**: `The mistake [role] make with [topic]`
- **Direct benefit**: `Get [specific result] in [timeframe]`
- **Social proof**: `[X] people are doing [thing]`
- **Urgency**: `[Offer] ends [timeframe]` (use sparingly — burns reputation)

### Body structure
1. **Hook** — first line decides if they read on
2. **Relevance** — why this matters to THEM specifically
3. **Story or proof** — concrete example
4. **Value delivery** — give before you ask
5. **Single CTA** — one action, one link

## Social Media

### LinkedIn (B2B)
- **Hook**: Counter-intuitive or controversial first line
- **Body**: 3–5 short paragraphs, white space matters
- **End**: Question that invites a comment
- **Cadence**: 3×/week beats daily noise

### Twitter / X
- **Thread (8–15 tweets)**: First tweet IS the hook — pay it off in tweet 2.
- **Single tweet**: Insight + one specific reason it matters.
- **Replies (3–5/day)**: Genuine value — earn reach by being useful in others' threads.
